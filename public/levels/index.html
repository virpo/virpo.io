Fossil record

The list is always 100. When a new level earns its place, an old one goes extinct and moves here.

extinct · 2023"You are a senior engineer with 20 years of experience."

Role-play preambles. Today's models don't need the pep talk; they need the context.

extinct · 2023"I'll tip you $200 for a perfect answer."

It sort of worked once. Nobody ever paid.

extinct · 2023"Take a deep breath and work on this step by step."

Reasoning models think before answering anyway.

extinct · 2023–24"Write the full code. No placeholders."

From the era of lazy models and "// rest of the code stays the same".

extinct · 2024.cursorrules

One file in the repo root. Replaced by rule folders and AGENTS.md, which every agent reads.

extinct · 2025Codex custom prompts

Deprecated in favour of skills.

endangered · 2025–"ultrathink"

Thinking keywords. Effort is a setting now, and reports disagree on whether the word still does anything.

extinct · 2024"How many r's are in strawberry?"

Retired as a benchmark. Still a good party trick.

How it works

Your level is how many of the 100 you do; the order decides your tier. Your agent reads check.md, runs a read-only probe that prints names and counts, judges each level, and opens this page with the result in the link after the #, which never reaches a server. What it can't see from your machine, you tick yourself, and your card says so.

Each level links to someone who does it, in their own words. Where good engineers disagree, both sides are there.

Who made this

Peter Hraska (@virpo). I spend an unhealthy amount of time with agents and talk to a lot of people about how they use them. "I use AI a lot" turned out to mean very different things, so I wrote down the tricks that are actually worth your time.

The whole list is open as Markdown and JSON. Wrong check, missing trick, one that should go extinct? Tell me on X.
